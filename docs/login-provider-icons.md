# Login provider icons

The Google, GitHub, Microsoft, and GitLab login icons are adapted from the
corresponding `flutter/assets/auth-*.svg` files in the RustDesk project:
https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk/tree/master/flutter/assets

RustDesk is distributed under AGPL-3.0. Provider names and marks identify their
respective services and remain the property of their respective owners. Unknown
or custom providers use a neutral text badge; remote SVG is never rendered.
